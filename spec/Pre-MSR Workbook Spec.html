How data flows

Raw extracts and driver tables feed two engine tables: Table1 on Consolidation RAW for the current year and Table19 on Y+1 for 2027. Every management sheet is a pivot or a SUMIFS/VLOOKUP view over one or both engines. The exception is IMS Stock, which sits upstream and drives the volume forecast.

Management sheets

The 10 black-tab sheets, in workbook order. "M" is this month's Pre-MSR (Oct) and "M-1" is last month's (Sep). Most of these sheets carry a Refresh_all macro button. Press it after the engines change so the pivots pick up new data.

#22026 DF , NNS ,Tons,Cases

Purpose
Full-year 2026 outlook per SKU: NNS in USD, tons and cases by month, quarter and FY.
Layout
Main_Cat › F-Group › SKU › Desc against Jan–Dec, Q1–Q4 and 2026 FY. NNS converted at the average exchange rate (117.3 SYP/USD).
Built from
3 pivots on Table1 (Consolidation RAW). Filters: FCST Type = M, YEARCUR = 2026, Active = 1, Country = Syria. The exchange rate comes from Assist.

Built from
2 pivots on Table1 (M-1 fields against M fields), plus table Table6. Feeds Summary for email.

Monthly refresh order

The order follows the dependencies above. The first three steps match the checklist on Dashboard.

  1. Update Assist: last actual month and exchange rates.
  2. Update SKU mapping-Pricing and the DSCR sheets. For new SKUs, follow Guide line for new SKU update.
  3. Move last month's raw data into the M-1 sheets, then paste the new NNSBreakDown-RAW and Sales Vs Activity-RAW extracts.
  4. Review IMS Stock and the Fayrouz arrivals. These drive FCST and FCST Y+1.
  5. Check the driver sheets: Price Chnge, BDA, Distribution by Month and Target Split.
  6. In Consolidation RAW and Y+1, copy M-1 values into the section marked "Copy M-1 before this section".
  7. Press Refresh_all, then run the navy check sheets against Mosaic.
  8. Review the 10 black sheets, then prepare the green Mosaic submission sheets.
